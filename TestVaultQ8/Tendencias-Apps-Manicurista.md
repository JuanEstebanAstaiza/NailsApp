## Features Tendencia 2025-2026

### ✅ Must-Have (Esenciales)
1. **Online Booking** - Página de reserva con link social, Google Reserve integration
2. **Automated Reminders** - SMS/Email reminders para reducir no-shows
3. **Calendar Sync** - Sincronización con otros calendarios evitando double-booking
4. **Payment Processing** - Integración de pagos (deposit collection)
5. **Client CRM** - Historial de clientas, notas, preferencias
6. **Mobile App** - iOS y Android para owners, staff y guests

### 🔥 Trending (En Tendencia)
1. **AI Receptionist** - Responde llamadas fuera de horario, texts missed callers automáticamente, books appointments sin staff involvement. Salones recuperan 35% de calls que irían unanswered cada mes.
2. **Missed Call Auto-Reply** - Cuando se pierde una llamada, auto-send text message al caller
3. **Web Chat Embeddable** - Chat web embebido en la página del salón
4. **Phone Call Integration** - Calls through desktop app con automatic phone notifications synced to client list and calendar
5. **Single Business Number** - Un número dedicado para texting y calling, protege personal time/privacy

### 📈 Revenue Impact (Impacto en Ingresos)
- **33% more revenue per guest** con online booking vs sin él (2025 Beauty and Wellness Benchmark Report)
- **35% of calls recovered** con AI Receptionist
- **25% checkout abandonment** cuando se fuerza account creation (Baymard Institute data)

### Para Nail Salons Multi-Staff (> 50 clientas)

- Payroll features

## Tendencias de UX/UI

1. **No forced account creation** - Clients should book without creating an account (25% abandonment rate)
2. **Express Booking™** - Send clients a digital link to complete info and confirm appointment (para phone bookings)
3. **Service customizations/add-ons** - Clients tailor experience while increasing ticket value
4. **Premium aesthetic** - GlossGenius es el más estéticamente pulido, Instagram-worthy booking experience
5. **Wallet passes** - Digital loyalty cards in Apple Wallet/Google Wallet (no separate app download)

---

## Tendencias de Marketing

1. **Automated review requests** - Built-in email/SMS campaigns
2. **Client marketplace** - Fresha y Vagaro tienen marketplaces para discovery de nuevos clientes
3. **Deal and package promotions** - Gift certificate management, promotional packages
4. **Livestreaming capabilities** - Virtual consultations (Vagaro)
5. **Referral rewards** - Clients share referral links from wallet card

---

## Tendencias de Fidelización (Ver [[Sistemas-Fidelizacion]])

1. **Digital punch cards** - Reemplazo de paper punch cards con digital versions en Apple/Google Wallet
2. **Points-based programs** - 1 point per $1 spent, points accumulate toward reward
3. **Tiered VIP programs** - Bronze/Silver/Gold tiers with progressively better perks
4. **Rebooking incentives** - Automated follow-up at exact moment nails are due (2-3 week cycle)
5. **Lapsed customer re-engagement** - Push notifications/SMS para clientes inactivos
