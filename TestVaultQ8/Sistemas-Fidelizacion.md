## Resumen Ejecutivo

Los sistemas de fidelización para manicuristas están diseñados para **aumentar la retención de clientas** y **reducir el churn**. La industria muestra que las top nail salons rebookan **69% de clientas** vs el promedio de **40%** - la diferencia son programas de fidelización personalizados.

## Formatos de Programas de Fidelización

### 1. Stamp Card / Punch Card (Tarjeta de Sellos)
**Formato más común en nail salons**

#### Cómo funciona:
- Clienta gana un sello/stamp por cada visita
- Después de X visitas, obtiene una recompensa

#### Estructura estándar para manicuristas:
| Servicio | Visitas necesarias | Recompensa |
|----------|-------------------|------------|
| Gel Manicure | 8 visits | 9th free (100% discount) |
| Pedicure | 6 visits | Free gel upgrade ($15 value) |
| Acrylic Fill | 10 visits | $20 off any service |

#### Estructura alternativa con descuento:
- Every 8th manicure: **15% discount**
- Every 10th visit: **20% discount**

#### Pros:
- Zero cost to set up (digital)
- Instantly understood by every client
- Creates a visible record the client carries with them

#### Cons:
- Physical cards get lost → Always keep backup in phone/app
- If client loses card after 7 visits, honour progress without friction builds more loyalty than reward itself

---

### 2. Points-Based Program (Programa de Puntos)
**Para salones con varied price points y service menus**

#### Cómo funciona:
- Client gana **1 point per $1 spent**
- Points accumulate toward a reward

#### Estructura ejemplo:
| Puntos | Recompensa | Valor |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| 50 points | Free nail art add-on | $15 value |
| 100 points | Free gel upgrade | $20 value |
| 200 points | Free deluxe pedicure | $55 value |

#### Pros:
- Flexible para varied price points
- Clientas pueden elegir recompensas según sus puntos
- Incentiva spending más alto por appointment

#### Cons:
- Más complejo de entender
- Requiere tracking más sofisticado

---

### 3. Tiered VIP Program (Programa de Tiers)
**Para established salons con loyal core client base**

#### Cómo funciona:
- Clientas suben de tier según spend threshold
- Cada tier tiene perks progressively better

#### Tiers ejemplo:
| Tier | Spend Threshold | Perks |
|------|----------------|-------|
| **Bronze** | $0+ | Standard point accrual, birthday reward |
| **Silver** | $500+ | Higher point accrual rate, 10% off retail products, priority booking |
| **Gold** | $1,000+ | Highest point accrual rate, 15% off retail products, early access to new services, exclusive client events |

#### Pros:
- Gamification element (clientas quieren subir de tier)
- Rewards high-value clients disproportionately
- Creates VIP feeling

#### Cons:
- High complexity to implement
- Requires significant data tracking
- May alienate lower-tier clients if not done carefully

---

### 4. Rebooking Incentive Program
**Específico para nail salons - built around natural 2-3 week service cycle**

#### Cómo funciona:
- Clienta rebook dentro de un window específico después de su última cita
- Obtains discount/reward por rebooking rápido

#### Estructura ejemplo:
| Window | Recompensa |
|--------|-----------|
| Within 7 days of last appointment | **10% discount** |
| Within 14 days | **5% discount** |
| After 21+ days | No reward (lapsed) |

#### Pros:
- Directly addresses the gap between appointments where most nail clients drift to competitor
- Automated follow-up at exact moment nails are due
- Removes the window of churn

#### Cons:
- Requires precise tracking of last appointment date
- Clientas que naturalmente rebookan cada 3-4 semanas no califican

---

### 5. Membership Subscription Program
**Para salones wanting recurring revenue predictability**

#### Cómo funciona:
- Clienta pays monthly fee for benefits like discounts and priority booking

#### Estructura ejemplo:
| Plan | Mensual | Benefits |
|------|---------|----------|
| Basic | $15/month | 10% off all services, priority booking |
| Premium | $25/month | 15% off all services, free nail art add-on monthly, priority booking |

#### Pros:
- Predictable recurring revenue
- Locks in client loyalty
- Higher perceived value for clientas regulares

#### Cons:
- Requires significant client trust upfront
- May not work for casual/occasional clients
- Complex to manage refunds/cancellations

## Delivery Methods (Métodos de Entrega)

### Paper Punch Cards (Traditional)
- **Dominated for years** pero declining en digital era
- Pros: Zero cost, instantly understood
- Cons: Lost easily, no automated reminders, no tracking analytics

### Mobile App Loyalty
- **Largely failed** - too many apps to download, low engagement
- Pros: Can include push notifications, rich features
- Cons: Download friction, low retention, high development cost

### Wallet Pass (Apple/Google Wallet) ⭐ Trending
- **Format gaining ground now** - digital loyalty card in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet
- No separate app to download
- Lives on same screen as boarding passes and payment cards
- Pros: Zero friction adoption, push notifications from wallet, no app maintenance
- Cons: Requires wallet pass integration (Cockato, FaveCard provide this)

### In-App Loyalty ⭐ Best for Custom Apps
- Built into your own booking/management app
- Pros: Full control over design and logic, integrated with booking system, can show progress bar, automated reminders within same app
- Cons: Requires building the app yourself

## Automatización de Fidelización (Key Features)

### 1. Auto-Track Appointments Count
- Cada appointment completado incrementa counter automáticamente
- Service filter opcional (solo contar ciertos servicios)

### 2. Progress Bar Visual
- Mostrar clientas cuánto les falta para recompensa
- "Faltan 3 citas para tu manicure gratis"

### 3. Automated Reminders When Close to Reward
- Cuando clienta está a 1-2 citas de recompensa, send reminder
- "¡Solo 2 citas más y tu próxima manicure es GRATIS!"

### 4. Lapsed Customer Re-engagement
- Clientas que no rebookan en X días → push notifications/SMS
- "We miss you! Come in for a special discount"

### 5. Reward Redemption at Checkout
- Cuando clienta califica, auto-apply discount al próximo appointment
- No need to remember to use punch card

## ROI de Fidelización (Return on Investment)

### Costos:
- **Paper punch cards**: ~$0.10-0.25 por card × 50 clientas = $5-12.50/mes
- **Digital loyalty app** (Cockato/FaveCard): Free up to 50 members, luego custom pricing
- **Built into own app**: Development time + maintenance

### Beneficios:
- **33% more revenue per guest** con online booking vs sin él
- Retention is cheaper than acquisition → cada clienta retenida = ~$100-200 saved vs acquiring new one
- Top nail salons rebook 69% vs average 40% → **29% improvement in retention rate**

## Implementación en Nuestra App

### Modelo: LoyaltyRule (Ver [[Arquitectura-App]])
```python
class LoyaltyRule(Base):
    client_id: UUID          # NULL para global rules
    type: str                # "punch-card" | "rebooking" | "points" | "tiered"
    trigger_count: int       # Número de citas necesarias
    service_filter: UUID     # NULL para todos los servicios
    reward_type: str         # "discount" | "free-service" | "upgrade"
    reward_value: float      # Porcentaje o valor de la recompensa
    rebooking_days: int      # Para rebooking rules (7 días)
```

### Endpoints:
- `POST /api/loyalty/rules/` - Crear regla
- `GET /api/loyalty/rules/` - Listar reglas
- `GET /api/loyalty/rules/{id}` - Obtener regla
- `PUT /api/loyalty/rules/{id}` - Actualizar regla
- `DELETE /api/loyalty/rules/{id}` - Eliminar regla
- `GET /api/loyalty/status/{client_id}` - Status de fidelización para clienta
